The Importance of the Amazon Rainforest

The Amazon is the largest tropical rainforest in the world, covering approximately 5.5 million square kilometers across South America. It is home to millions of species of plants and animals and plays an important role in regulating the Earth's climate. The forest absorbs carbon dioxide, stores carbon, and helps maintain rainfall patterns throughout the region.
